# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public-facing International Property Management marketing, property, services, news, and Insights pages.
- Public informational and editorial routes rendered by the application.

## Out of scope
- Authenticated owner portal and internal API/dashboard flows.
- Standalone owner proposal pages unless they are intentionally public marketing destinations.

## Target audience
- Property owners seeking luxury vacation-rental management in Playa del Carmen, Tulum, Lake Norman, and related markets.
- Vacation-rental hosts seeking educational guidance and market updates.

## Primary keywords
- Vacation rental property management
- Luxury vacation rental management
- Playa del Carmen property management
- Tulum vacation rental management
- Lake Norman vacation rental management
- Airbnb hosting and vacation-rental operations

## Crawler assumptions
- Public routes should be discoverable by Google, social preview bots, and AI crawlers.
- The Vite React SPA should provide source-visible crawlable metadata/content for public pages, or use an SSR/SSG equivalent.
