Do not use older blanket “no setup fee” or “no-cost exit” marketing language to override the owner's newer listing ownership FAQ instructions. A FAQ-only request does not authorize rewriting signed agreements, pricing systems, or onboarding workflows.

**Why:** The owner supplied a new bilingual FAQ brief distinguishing listing ownership options and qualifying cancellation by the signed agreement. Older public marketing and onboarding explanations predate those distinctions.

**How to apply:** Keep FAQs consistent with the owner's approved brief and retain signed-agreement qualifications. If later aligning onboarding or contractual documents, confirm the current agreement rather than treating legacy website copy as the contract. Preserve independent owner tax responsibility and secure co-host access guidance.
