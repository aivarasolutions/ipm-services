---
name: Onboarding credential safety
description: Security boundary for Airbnb account access during IPM client onboarding.
---

Never collect, store, or email an Airbnb password through the IPM onboarding form. Use Airbnb co-host invitations or a separately coordinated secure-access method instead.

**Why:** Email and ordinary form handling are not appropriate channels for third-party account passwords. A leaked credential could expose the client’s Airbnb account and reservations.

**How to apply:** Preserve the no-password notice and acknowledgment in future onboarding changes. Admin notifications may include the Airbnb account identifier, but must never include an account password.
