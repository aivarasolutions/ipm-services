name: Archived owner proposals
description: Why completed proposal and checklist pages remain reachable even though they are no longer promoted.
---

Completed owner proposals and the Tegucigalpa launch checklist should stay off the public Insights navigation, but their direct URLs may remain available as archives unless the user explicitly asks to delete the pages.

**Why:** The user said those proposals had passed and wanted them removed from the Insights tab. Preserving direct links avoids breaking existing bookmarks or past correspondence while meeting that navigation request.

**How to apply:** Do not re-add these completed items to desktop or mobile navigation. If the user later requests permanent deletion or redirect behavior, clarify the desired fate of existing direct URLs before removing routes.
