---
name: Furnishing service policy
description: User-defined commercial boundaries for IPM's standalone furnishing and setup service.
---

Furnishing, sourcing and setup are paid standalone services available to both
management clients and nonclients. They must not be bundled into the existing
listing/full-management commissions or advertised as a free management benefit.

**Why:** The user explicitly defined an independent revenue-generating service,
not a change to IPM's management programs.

**How to apply:** Keep management enrollment optional, purchasing approvals
written, IPM fees separate from vendor expenses, and the sourcing/setup
percentage fees alternative rather than stacked. Delivery, shipping, duties,
taxes and third-party labor are excluded from the percentage base.

Do not promise booking/occupancy/revenue improvements, present illustrative
interiors as completed client projects, or activate paid checkout until pricing
terms, policies and the payment flow are confirmed.

**Why:** These were explicit constraints in the user's furnishing-service brief.

**How to apply:** Treat calculations as estimates and signed quotations and
service agreements as the controlling engagement documents. Physical setup
depends on local representative availability; IPM does not finance purchases.
