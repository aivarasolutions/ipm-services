---
name: Education editorial voice
description: Operator-style education without invented IPM experiences or unsupported claims.
---

IPM educational content should sound like an experienced property manager explaining the real work, not corporate promotional copy. Do not invent first-person incidents, IPM case studies, statistics or testimonials to create that voice. Use practical explanations and explicitly illustrative examples; actual IPM experience stories need approved supporting facts.

**Why:** The user's education brief asks for a credible, experienced conversational voice while explicitly prohibiting fabricated experience, thin promotional articles and unsupported outcomes.

**How to apply:** Apply this to articles, translations, community resources and future topic expansion. Leave material needing factual or specialist confirmation in draft rather than filling gaps with plausible claims.
